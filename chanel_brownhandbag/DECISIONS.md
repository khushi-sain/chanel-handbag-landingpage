DECISIONS

## 1. Why this ingestion strategy over the obvious alternative?
I used the exact product image supplied by the brief as the single handbag asset and built the landing experience around it. This was preferable to sourcing multiple online images because it keeps the product presentation consistent, avoids unnecessary external dependencies, and preserves the single-product requirement.

## 2. One trade-off under the time limit
I prioritized the first-screen visual impact, responsive layout, interaction polish, and animation instead of implementing a full commerce backend. With a real week, I would add production-grade accessibility testing, optimized image delivery, a complete mobile navigation state, checkout/account flows, analytics, and performance profiling.

## 3. Where did you use AI tools?
AI was used to accelerate the initial HTML/CSS/Vanilla JS implementation and generate ideas for luxury motion, responsive structure, micro-interactions, and the easter egg. I personally verified and changed the product asset, single-product scope, visual palette, logo treatment, responsive behavior, animations, CTA interaction, and final page structure.
